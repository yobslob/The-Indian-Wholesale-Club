# IWC Launch Regions: The Homesickness Catalogue (v2)

**Regions:** Delhi · Punjab · Rajasthan · Assam · Maharashtra · Kerala
**Researched:** 1 October 2026 · replaces v1
**Goal:** list *everything* a person from each state would miss: what they wore and what was in the kitchen. Nothing is left out because it's also made elsewhere. If it feels like that state to people from there, it's in.

---

## How to read this

Every state has the same layout:

1. **Launch picks:** 12 items to list first. These are the most recognisable and the easiest to explain.
2. **Clothing:** women · men · kids · bridal & festive · accessories & footwear · fabrics & crafts.
3. **Pantry:** masalas & spices · pickles (achar) · chutneys · papad, wadi & badi · namkeen & snacks · sweets · grains & staples · tea, drinks & other.

Columns: **Item · What it is · Where it's from** (city or district when known; "statewide" otherwise) · **Note** (GI = registered Geographical Indication · Festival = when people buy it · Trend = current style).

GI names ("Kota Doria", "Paithani", "Bikaneri Bhujia" and so on) belong to the real product, so use them only for the genuine thing. Everything else can be named and styled however works best on the site.

## Appendix: which of these can actually ship to the US

Everything above stays in the catalogue. This is only a note on what each type needs before it can be sold (Q-10, D-032):

| Type | Examples | What it needs |
|---|---|---|
| Clothing, footwear, accessories | Everything in the clothing sections | Origin label + "Imported" (D-004). Straightforward. |
| Dry spices, masalas, flours, rice, tea, dry namkeen, papad, chips | Bhujia, chivda, papad, masalas, Joha rice | FDA-registered maker, Prior Notice per shipment, US-style ingredient/allergen label |
| Pickles and chutneys in oil or vinegar | Achar, lonche, thecha | As above. Acidified pickles may also need an FDA process filing, so check each maker |
| Milk sweets | Kalakand, peda, mawa kachori, ghewar | Shelf life and dairy rules; choose long-life, packaged versions only |
| Fish pickles | Kerala fish / prawn pickle | FDA seafood rules (HACCP) |
| Meat pickles (beef, pork, chicken) | Kerala beef achar and similar | Meat products fall under USDA, not FDA; treat as not shippable unless a customs broker confirms otherwise |
| Fresh produce, alcohol | Tezpur litchi, fresh kaji nemu, rice beers | Out of scope |
| Jewellery (gold/silver) | Assamese gohona, Rajputi sets | Separate product type; a founder decision |
