What to look at

  • Use the floating bar at the bottom to switch between Home · Region · Product · Admin listing. Motion: on/off shows what visitors with “reduce motion” see.
  • Home: the photo hero (scroll slowly: the brand name fades out word by word as the logo fades in at the top left) → Just listed → Pick your home. Resting the pointer on a state’s name (or stamp) for 700 ms darkens that state on the map; hovering the map itself reacts at once.
  • Region: the hero → filter pills → New arrivals → Most wanted → the album (a mosaic that moves by itself, cannot be touched) → Curated for you (one card) → Leaving soon.
  • Product: one full-length photo (never taller than the screen) with three stacked beside it (click one to swap), the heart beside the name, Add to bag, Details and Size chart with + / −, then Reviews, Similar items and Curated for you in smaller cards.
  • Everything marked is a placeholder: photos are drawn patterns labelled “Placeholder”, dotted-underlined prices, dates, counts and ratings are samples, dashed “To be written” boxes are text still to come (the founder’s copy, the listing, real reviews), and greetings are the seeded drafts (D-019).

Fallbacks (D-052): Helvetica Neue is built into Apple devices only; elsewhere (including this Windows machine) the page uses TeX Gyre Heros, a free Helvetica look-alike. Georgia is missing on Android; there the page uses Gelasio. Each fallback downloads only where it is needed.

The India map

Drawn from DataMeet India’s state boundaries (CC BY 4.0), whose Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh were updated to the Survey of India map in 2021: J&K includes Gilgit-Baltistan and Ladakh includes Aksai Chin, as on the reference map the founder shared. All 36 regions, one static SVG, about 27 KB gzipped, no map library. The licence needs a visible credit (“Map data: DataMeet India, CC BY 4.0”), shown under the map and in the footer. Rebuild with node design/mockups/shared/build-map.mjs <folder>.
