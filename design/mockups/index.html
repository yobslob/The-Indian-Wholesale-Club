How to look at them

  • Open a direction and use the floating bar at the bottom to switch between Home · Region · Product · Admin listing. Motion: on/off previews what visitors with “reduce motion” turned on see (no smooth scroll, no parallax, images simply visible).
  • Try it at phone width too (narrow the window). Keyboard: Tab moves through links; the jump links on the region page scroll to Clothing / Spices.
  • They need an internet connection (Google Fonts and Lenis load from a CDN). Double-clicking the files works; so does python -m http.server 4321 --directory design/mockups.
  • Everything marked is a placeholder: photos are drawn patterns labelled “Placeholder” (no stock photos); prices, dates and counts with a dotted underline are samples; dashed “To be written” boxes are the founder’s text (C2); greetings are the seeded drafts (D-019). The India map is a labelled box (see Q-21 below).

Shared by all three

  • Motion (the D-049 guard rails): Lenis smooth scroll (about 4 KB gzipped), reveal by clipping + slow scale + fade, parallax and a slight sideways drift from scroll speed, all lighter on phones and off with “reduce motion”. Content is readable with JavaScript off. In the real build this is one small client module loaded after the page is interactive; fonts are self-hosted with next/font, not loaded from Google.
  • Script greetings: the Malayalam greeting uses Noto Sans Malayalam, subset to just that word and loaded only on the Kerala page (design.md §3). That is also why Home shows region names and not greetings: 36 greetings would load many script fonts on the first page.
  • Product photos: 4 : 5 portrait is proposed for every product card and the first gallery photo (clothing is photographed standing or draped). Gallery and editorial images vary.
  • Admin Listing is the same plain, dense screen in all three; only the tokens change. Items tagged “target” (camera photos, suggested price, quantity re-checks) are coding-phase work (C3, C6).

What the founder decides

  1. Which direction: A, B or C, or a mix (say which parts, e.g. “B’s region page with A’s colours”).
  2. Dark mode on customer pages (design.md §7): choosing C makes the store dark. Choosing A or B keeps it light. Offering both would need two accent colours per region (one for light, one for dark), because an accent that reads on ivory is too dark on black.
  3. Type pairing: keep the direction’s pair, or swap (all six fonts are free, open licence).
  4. Product photo shape: 4 : 5 portrait as proposed, or another ratio. The COO will shoot every product this way (C3).
  5. The India map source (Q-21): the map must follow India’s official boundaries. Which source should the SVG be traced from?
